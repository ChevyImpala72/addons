## About

The Energy Monitoring add-on provides a comprehensive dashboard for tracking your home's energy usage. It displays real-time energy flow between solar panels, battery storage, grid connection, and home consumption.

### Features

- Real-time energy flow visualization
- Solar production monitoring
- Battery state of charge tracking
- Grid import/export statistics
- Daily, weekly, and monthly energy charts
- Cost estimation based on your energy price
- Self-sufficiency calculation
- Dark/light theme support
- Responsive design for mobile devices

## Installation

1. Navigate to your Home Assistant instance.
2. Go to **Settings** > **Add-ons** > **Add-on Store**.
3. Find the "Energy Monitoring" add-on and click it.
4. Click on the **INSTALL** button.
