# Changelog

## 1.0.0

### Added

- Initial release
- Real-time energy flow visualization
- Solar production monitoring
- Battery state of charge tracking
- Grid import/export statistics
- Daily, weekly, and monthly energy charts
- Cost estimation based on configurable energy price
- Self-sufficiency calculation
- Dark/light/auto theme support
- Responsive design for mobile devices
- Configurable update interval
- Option to show/hide solar, battery, and grid sections
